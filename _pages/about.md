<section class="home-section">
  <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
  <h2>Data-centric deep learning, applied to bioacoustics</h2>
  <p class="section-lead">My research focuses on <strong>data-centric deep learning methods</strong>, particularly self-supervised and active learning approaches. I work at the intersection of machine learning and bioacoustics, developing foundation models and large-scale benchmarks that advance both fields.</p>

  <div class="cards">
    <div class="card">
      <p class="card__index">01</p>
      <h3>Self-Supervised Learning</h3>
      <p>Audio representation learning — masked autoencoders and probing methods for multi-label classification.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <p class="card__index">02</p>
      <h3>Active Learning</h3>
      <p>Human-in-the-loop deep learning: query strategies, embedding quality, and label-efficient training.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="card">
      <p class="card__index">03</p>
      <h3>Bioacoustics</h3>
      <p>Foundation models and large-scale benchmarks that make avian bioacoustics a frontier domain for deep learning.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <p class="section-note">My goal is to establish bioacoustics as a frontier domain for deep learning research while translating state-of-the-art methods into practical tools for conservation and environmental monitoring. I am committed to building infrastructure for reproducible science through unified evaluation frameworks and open-source resources.</p>
</section>
