## Research Interests

My research focuses on **data-centric deep learning methods**, particularly self-supervised and active learning approaches. I work at the intersection of machine learning and bioacoustics, developing foundation models and large-scale benchmarks that advance both fields. 

My goal is to establish bioacoustics as a frontier domain for deep learning research while translating state-of-the-art methods into practical tools for conservation and environmental monitoring. I am committed to building infrastructure for reproducible science through unified evaluation frameworks and open-source resources.
